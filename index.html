Layer 1 · Go-Live Readiness

Start with what's blocking the launch.

Go-Live Readiness puts every workstream, open risk, and next action on one screen. A cross-functional team can see what still has to happen before anyone makes the go or no-go call.

Open Go-Live Readiness
Layer 2 · Mapper and Studio

Decide what's worth the money.

Modernization Mapper sorts a company's applications into keep, evolve, replace, or retire by business value and technical health. Prioritization Studio scores roadmap ideas on reach, impact, confidence, and effort.

Open the Mapper
Layer 3 · Chicago 'L' tracker

Map the whole system first.

The 'L' tracker draws every CTA line and station on an 8-bit map of Chicago and runs trains along the real routes. It's built to switch to live train positions once the CTA feed is connected.

Open the map
Layer 4 · Ledger Leagues

Then ship something people use.

Ledger Leagues runs private NFL leagues. Each week everyone gets 1,000 virtual credits to bet on head-to-head picks, and whoever ends the week with the bigger return wins the matchup.
